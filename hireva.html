Privacy & data transparency

Your practice history lives on your device, not on our server

Hireva is a client-side application. We do not run a user database, do not collect personal data, and do not embed secret API keys in this file. Your answers, history and favorites are stored in your browser and never leave it unless you choose to export them.

Local-first storage

Interviews, answers, favorites and progress are saved in your browser via localStorage. Export or wipe them anytime.

Microphone consent

Voice mode asks permission each session, captures speech-to-text only on this page, and never records audio.

AI processing disclosure

If you connect an AI key, only your prompts are sent to that provider. We never auto-send answers in the background.

About Hireva

Hireva is a client-side interview preparation platform. The role database is built from publicly available occupational data (O*NET 31.x competencies and tasks, ESCO v1.2 skill/occupation taxonomy, U.S. OPM structured-interview methodology, India NCS career references). Interview questions are original Hireva content written to be competency-linked and rubric-scored. Hireva provides practice guidance only — it does not make hiring decisions and is not affiliated with any employer.
