Safety Tips

    Why Scams Work

    Understanding the psychology helps you resist the next attempt.

    🧠 How scammers manipulate people

    Scammers do not rely on technology alone. They rely on human emotions:

    • Urgency – “Act within 24 hours or lose everything”
    • Fear – “Your account will be blocked / police will arrest you”
    • Greed – “You have won ₹5 lakh / guaranteed 40% returns”
    • Authority – Impersonating bank, police, customs, government
    • Scarcity – “Only 2 slots left / limited time offer”

    When these emotions are triggered, the thinking part of the brain is partially bypassed. That is why even intelligent people fall for scams.

    📦 How fake courier / customs scams work

    You receive an SMS that a parcel is held for incomplete address or customs duty. The link opens a perfect-looking tracking page. You pay a small fee and enter card details. The page steals your data. Sometimes a follow-up call from a “customs officer” escalates into a digital-arrest demand for large sums.

    🔐 How OTP scams work

    Scammer already has your phone number and partial bank details. They call pretending to be the bank’s fraud team. They say a transaction is pending and ask you to “read out the OTP you just received to cancel it”. The OTP is actually authorizing a new transaction or adding a beneficiary. Money leaves within seconds.

    💸 How UPI & QR scams work

    You are selling something online. Buyer says “I am sending money, just approve the request” or sends a QR code “to receive payment”. Approving the request or scanning the QR actually debits your account. Golden rule: you never enter UPI PIN to receive money.

    📞 How call-forwarding scams work

    A “delivery agent” or “bank executive” asks you to dial a code such as *21*9876543210#. This activates unconditional call forwarding. All future calls — including bank OTP voice calls — silently go to the scammer. They then empty your accounts. Defence: never dial codes from strangers; if in doubt dial ##002#.

    🖥️ How remote-access scams work

    Caller claims to be tech support or bank security. They instruct you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or UltraViewer “so they can fix the issue”. Once connected they can see your screen, approve transactions, change passwords and transfer money while you watch helplessly.
