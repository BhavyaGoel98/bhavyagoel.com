Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Where is my information stored?

In this browser’s local storage on this device. It is not synced or backed up automatically. Use the JSON backup button regularly.

Does TripNest collect parent signatures?

No. Permission statuses are manual notes about paper slips. Follow your school’s approval process and legal requirements.

Can I try it before adding my own data?

Yes. Explore the read-only sample demo. Demo records are separate from your workspace and never saved into it.

Can I move my data to another browser?

Export a JSON backup and import it in the other browser. Import replaces that browser’s current TripNest workspace after a confirmation.
