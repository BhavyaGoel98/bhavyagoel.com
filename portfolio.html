About Bhavya

I'm Bhavya Goel — a 10-year-old coder, AI generalist, and tech enthusiast from India. I taught myself to code websites and master advanced AI models like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

My builder journey began with starting Bhavy Website Solutions, where I designed and delivered custom websites for clients. While running it, I founded my brand BG Fame for AI education — and after completing my client deliverables, I shut down Bhavy Website Solutions to focus fully on generative AI.

Through BG Fame, I actively teach other people how to effectively use AI tools through workshops, curated frameworks, and masterclasses. (BG Fame is dedicated strictly to AI education & workshops; it does not build AI tools.)
