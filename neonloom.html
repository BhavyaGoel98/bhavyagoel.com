How NeonLoom Works

Engineered for smooth, low-latency optical tracking and expressive audiovisual feedback across desktop and mobile browsers.

01. Optical Hand Pose

21-Point Skeletal Mapping

MediaPipe Hands locates 21 3D landmarks per hand on every new video frame. Velocity-adaptive smoothing eliminates resting jitter while keeping fast hand motion crisp.

02. Crossing & Identity Lock

Temporal Continuity Assignment

Hands are tracked using mirror-aware handedness, palm geometry, and Hungarian trajectory matching—so crossing your wrists or using a single hand never swaps colors or snaps strings.

03. Elastic String Physics

Pentatonic Tension Synthesis

Seven elastic cradle threads connect matching fingertips and thumb-to-pinky diagonals. Stretching increases pitch and shifts hue from cyan to magenta to solar yellow.

How to Position Your Hands

NeonLoom adapts seamlessly whether you raise one hand, two hands, or cross them in motion.

Two-Hand Cat's Cradle Mode

Hold both hands 30–60 cm from the camera with palms facing the screen or slightly angled toward each other. Five parallel threads link matching fingertips while two diagonal threads cross from thumb to pinky.

· Spread hands apart to increase string tension and raise pitch
· Quick finger flicks pluck individual threads with harmonic waves
· Cross hands horizontally—identity lock preserves Left (Cyan) and Right (Magenta)

Single-Hand Solo Loom Mode

When only one hand is visible in frame, NeonLoom identifies whether it is your Left (Cyan) or Right (Magenta) hand and automatically weaves an intra-hand harmonic fan across your five fingertips.

· Open and pinch your thumb and fingers to pluck solo chords
· Brief tracking interruptions coast smoothly for 180ms without flicker
· Raise your second hand at any time to transition into the full two-hand cradle

Camera Permission & Local Privacy

NeonLoom processes 100% of camera frames locally inside your browser using WebAssembly and WebGL. Video frames are never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any server.

· Requires browser camera permission when starting Camera Mode
· Stop or switch cameras at any time with full hardware track cleanup
· If no camera is available, use the built-in Interactive Simulator

Procedural Audio & Device Compatibility

All sound is synthesized in real time using the Web Audio API—no external audio files required. Audio starts only after your explicit click gesture to respect browser autoplay policies.

· Desktop: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari with webcam or Simulator
· Mobile: iOS Safari & Android Chrome with Front/Rear camera switch
· Performance Tiers: High, Balanced, and Eco modes for mobile battery life

NeonLoom Guide & Controls

Two-Hand Cat's Cradle: Raise both hands to weave 7 elastic threads between your fingertips. Move hands apart to tighten tension (Cyan → Magenta → Yellow) and flick fingers to pluck harmonic notes.

Single-Hand Solo Loom: Raise either your Left (Cyan) or Right (Magenta) hand alone to weave intra-hand threads between your thumb and fingers.

Hand Crossing & Fast Motion: Our temporal tracking engine locks onto each hand's trajectory and handedness so crossing your hands horizontally will not swap their colors or snap threads.

Camera Permission Troubleshooting: If camera access was denied, click the camera icon in your browser address bar to allow camera permissions, then click Restart Camera—or enable Simulator Mode to weave with your mouse or touchscreen.
