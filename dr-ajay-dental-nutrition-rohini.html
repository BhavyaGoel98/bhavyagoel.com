Two practitioners

One clinic, two disciplines that keep correcting each other.

Most dental problems have a dietary half, and most diet plans have a dental consequence. Here you don't get referred across the city to find that out.

Treatments

What actually happens in the chair.

RCT

Root canal treatment

Infected pulp removed, canal sealed, tooth kept. Including re-treatment of root canals that failed elsewhere.

IMP

Dental implants

A titanium root and a fitted crown for a gap that would otherwise shift every tooth beside it.

ORT

Braces & aligners

Conventional metal braces or clear aligners, planned around how long you're actually willing to wear them.

RST

Fillings & crowns

Tooth-coloured restorations and zirconia or ceramic crowns, matched to the teeth on either side.

HYG

Scaling & polishing

Cleaning below the gumline — the appointment that prevents most of the others on this page.

AES

Veneers & whitening

Cosmetic correction for chips, stains and spacing, with an honest word about what enamel can take.

SUR

Extractions

Including impacted wisdom teeth, done with enough anaesthesia and enough time.

NUT

Diet counselling

Condition-led nutrition plans — diabetes, deficiencies, recovery — reviewed rather than printed once and forgotten.
